Who I am

I'm a Senior QA Engineer with 10+ years building test automation strategies from the ground up — across fintech, education, and enterprise software. Currently I own the full QA strategy for KYC, customer onboarding, and identity verification flows, designing frameworks that span E2E, component, API, mobile, and performance layers.

I care deeply about quality culture: I founded QA School in 2019 to train early-career QA professionals, and I created three custom QA metrics — PPI, SRM, and RBD — that I've applied across 27 sprints to measure real-world quality trends beyond simple pass/fail rates.

I work remotely in English, collaborating across Brazil, Canada, and Ukraine, and I hold a C1 Advanced English certification. Currently pursuing two Post-Graduate Diplomas at CESAR School: DevOps and Agile Testing.
